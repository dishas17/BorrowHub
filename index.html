About BorrowHub

BorrowHub is a college campus resource-sharing platform engineered to simplify academic life. We empower university students to lend and borrow essential belongings — including engineering drafters, scientific calculators, laboratory coats, textbooks, and sports equipment — 100% free of charge within our campus community.

🎁

100% Free Sharing

Students share academic items without any rental fees. Help each other succeed during lab exams and mid-terms.

🔒

Trusted Campus Peers

Every user is verified via College ID and departmental enrollment. Handover happens safely on campus.

🛡️

Moderated Platform

Campus coordinators have dedicated oversight tools to review listings and maintain institutional safety.
